# Privacy Policy (RecipeHub)

**Last updated:** 2026-02-14

RecipeHub is an educational project. This Privacy Policy explains what data may be processed when you use the application.

## 1. Data Controller

The project author (see `README.md`) is responsible for the processing of personal data within this educational project.

## 2. What data we collect

Depending on how you use the application, RecipeHub may process:

- **Account data:** email address and authentication-related data (for sign-in / session management).
- **Content data:** recipes, ingredients and other user-generated content you create.
- **Technical data:** basic request information (e.g., IP address, user agent) that can appear in server logs.

## 3. Cookies and similar technologies

RecipeHub uses cookies for:

- **Essential cookies** (required): authentication/session and security features.
- **Preferences cookies** (optional): user preferences such as cookie consent choice.

RecipeHub does not intentionally use marketing cookies. Analytics cookies may be used only if explicitly enabled in the future.

## 4. Purpose and legal basis

Personal data may be processed to:

- Provide core functionality (authentication, storing and managing recipes/ingredients).
- Ensure security and prevent abuse.
- Store your cookie consent selection.

Legal basis: **legitimate interest** (educational project operation) and **consent** (for optional cookies/preferences).

## 5. Data retention

- Account and content data may be stored while your account exists.
- Technical logs may be stored for a limited period for debugging and security.
- Cookie consent is stored until you clear cookies or change your preferences.

## 6. Data sharing

RecipeHub does not sell personal data. Data is not shared with third parties, except:

- Infrastructure providers required to run the project (e.g., hosting/database) if applicable.

## 7. User rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request:

- Access, correction, or deletion of your data.
- Restriction or objection to processing.
- Withdrawal of consent for optional cookies.

For educational projects, responses may be limited by technical constraints.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect data, but no method of transmission/storage is 100% secure.

## 9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the author listed in `README.md`.
